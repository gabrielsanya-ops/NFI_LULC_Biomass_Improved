# Assumptions and open questions

## Assumptions (documented)

1. Shrub allometric `log` is treated as natural log (`ln`). The Word document does not state the base.
2. Total biomass = AGB + BGB. Used by the carbon formula; not written as an explicit equation in the Word document.
3. Wood density unit treated as Mg/m3 (conventional with volume in m3 giving Mg).
4. Land-use proportions use Final LULC from the LULC Validation baseline (one label per office plot).
5. Sampling Unit Area is not stated numerically in the calculation document, so SU totals that require area are not computed.
